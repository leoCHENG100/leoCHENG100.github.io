Evaluation scope & caveats

Evaluated on two benchmarks with DeepSeek-v3.2 and Gemini-3-Flash. DeepSeek EHRSQL success rises from 41.65% to 47.68%, with 242 → 64 persistent entries. Its LoCoMo overall F1 / BLEU-4 rises from 16.59 / 5.36 to 21.83 / 6.54 versus A-MEM.

Fewer entries do not necessarily mean fewer tokens, lower storage cost, or lower context cost. NeuroMem does not lead every LoCoMo category, and the main single-run comparisons do not establish statistical significance. Numbers here follow the supplied paper, Section 4.2, rather than the earlier conference-page abstract.
